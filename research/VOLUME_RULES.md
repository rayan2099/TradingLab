# Volume rules

- Volume is meaningful only with price location and subsequent confirmation.
- A high-volume candle after decline can become positive after a close above its high.
- A high-volume candle after rise becomes negative after a close below its low.
- Gap candles are measured from the previous close where the teacher explicitly says so.
- Mod uses MA20 for early-entry experiments and MA50 for the momentum-pullback filter.
- Auction volume, “very high” volume, and relative-volume thresholds remain uncalibrated.
