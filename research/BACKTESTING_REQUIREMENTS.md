# Backtesting requirements

- Point-in-time Saudi OHLCV including auction prints, corporate actions, delistings, and suspensions.
- TASI point-in-time series and sector membership.
- No look-ahead in zones, pivots, divergence, or top-gainer selection.
- Separate teacher rules from every Mod experiment.
- Report trade count, expectancy, hit rate, payoff ratio, MAE, MFE, drawdown, regime, and slippage.
- Walk-forward calibration for qualitative thresholds; untouched out-of-sample evaluation.
- Failure and cancelled setups must be included, not only published winners.
