# Risk management rules

- Course stop: no more than 3% from entry.
- Stop is evaluated on a close where specified; a wick alone is not automatically final failure.
- False-stop-break handling must not silently enlarge portfolio risk.
- Teacher target baseline: 2R–3R or the next valid technical obstacle.
- Mod distributes capital across roughly three stocks and reduces size or abstains in a weak TASI.
- The repository's existing 5% bot stop is an engineering safety ceiling, not a teacher trading rule.
