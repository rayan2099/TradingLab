# Teacher knowledge base — أحمد (أبو عبدالله)

## Decision framework

1. Read the market and the stock on a higher timeframe.
2. Identify a defensible market-maker zone; the zone is permission, not an entry.
3. Move to 15 minutes (preferred) or 5 minutes only when momentum justifies it.
4. Require a completed bullish pattern and a known stop no wider than 3%.
5. Check the opposing zone, TASI, news, and negative divergence before entry.
6. Manage the trade on a smaller timeframe; exit or trail when a bearish pattern appears.

## Confirmed principles

- Buy zones: positivity achieved or negativity cancelled.
- Sell zones: negativity achieved or positivity cancelled.
- Intraday entry follows a completed pattern inside a valid zone.
- The bullish engulfing is a single-decision candle at its close.
- Pattern stop distance must not exceed 3%.
- False stop breaks use the low of the stop-breaking candle as a second closing line.
- Volume is interpreted by location and subsequent closes, not magnitude alone.
- A beautiful lower-timeframe pattern may be rejected by higher-timeframe supply or bearish evidence.
