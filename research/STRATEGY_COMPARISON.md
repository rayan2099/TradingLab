# Teacher vs Mod

| Dimension | Ahmed Abu Abdullah | Mod |
|---|---|---|
| Entry timing | Completed intraday pattern | May anticipate on 30m or special direct retests |
| Primary edge | Zone + pattern + context | Same base plus momentum/volume screening |
| Indicators | Minimal, contextual | Adds RSI, Fibonacci, volume MAs, stochastic |
| Risk | Pattern stop ≤3% | Same base, plus scaling, partial exits, small size in weak markets |
| Weak market | Avoid or require strong context | Often abstain; otherwise very small size and 1–1.5% targets |
| Rule authority | Course/teacher source | Practitioner rules and experiments |

Agreement on one successful example does not promote the whole Mod strategy to a course rule.
