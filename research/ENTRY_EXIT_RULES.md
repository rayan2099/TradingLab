# Entry and exit rules

## Entry

- Do not enter merely because price reached a zone.
- Most patterns trigger on a closing confirmation; bullish engulfing is decided at its own close.
- Split entry or wait for retest when the stop is relatively wide.
- Mod's anticipatory 30m and direct gap entries are separate experiments.

## Exit

- Exit on a confirmed bearish intraday pattern, close below a volume candle, or final stop failure.
- Sell before nearby resistance when the technical target is obstructed.
- Partial exits may be bought back only after a new bullish pattern.
