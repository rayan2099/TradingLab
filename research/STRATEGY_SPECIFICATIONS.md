# Strategy specifications

## Core course strategy

Higher-timeframe zone → intraday pattern → closing confirmation → stop ≤3% → 2R/3R or next
resistance → trail on a smaller timeframe.

## Mod momentum pullback

- Candidate: ≥12% rise over 3–7 candles.
- Volume: breakout over volume MA50; exact breakout definition needs calibration.
- Correction: M-like, not necessarily symmetric.
- Trigger: daily buyer with volume or completed 30m bullish pattern.
- Enhancers: positive divergence, Fibonacci 38.2/50/61.8, moving average.
- Failure: no buyer appears, or a completed bearish structure remains uncancelled.

## Mod volume-MA20

Inside demand, use the first green candle whose volume crosses above volume MA20; enter on a
close above its high. This is an early-entry experiment and needs independent testing.

## Mod gap-up retest

Direct entry at the lower edge of the gap candle on the first return, with a close below the candle
as failure. This conflicts with the core requirement for an intraday pattern and must be tested
separately.
