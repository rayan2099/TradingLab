# Market filters

- TASI at weekly supply: avoid new longs.
- TASI and stock both at demand: patterns have better contextual support.
- Negative higher timeframes can override a lower-timeframe false break.
- Geopolitical and company-news risk must be checked.
- Avoid holding overnight before earnings announcements.
- First-hour traded value around SAR 1bn was a teacher heuristic, not a timeless constant; calibrate by regime.
