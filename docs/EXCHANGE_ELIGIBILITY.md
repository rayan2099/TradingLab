# Exchange eligibility: NOT VERIFIED

Phase 1 uses Binance **public** market data only. Downloading candles and paper
trading need no account, no KYC and no keys.

Before any live trading (Phase 4) someone must verify, from primary sources:

- Whether the chosen exchange serves residents of Saudi Arabia, and on what terms.
- Saudi rules on crypto-asset trading by individuals (SAMA / CMA position at that time).
- Funding routes (SAR deposits/withdrawals), fees, and tax/zakat implications.
- That the exchange is supported by Freqtrade for spot trading.

Nothing in this repo assumes the answer. Until this file says otherwise, live trading stays off.
